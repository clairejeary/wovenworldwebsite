ABOUT

Woven World was created out of a love for beautiful buildings and the joy of making.


Our collection of mini tapestry kits celebrates Sussex landmarks in a manageable format for both beginners and seasoned stitchers.


Master one simple stitch


Use our tapestry canvas and carefully selected colours


It’s painting with wool


Each kit contains everything you need and is compact enough to go wherever your creativity takes you – on a bus, on a train, or a plane:

  • Appletons 100% English Tapestry Wool
  • Colour printed 100% Cotton canvas
  • Colour chart with full instructions
  • Tapestry needle


Once completed your design can be framed, used to adorn a small pillow, or to cover a journal
